# Business Systems

A business system connects the processes that keep an organization running.

## Common Systems

- CRM
- Booking systems
- Inventory management
- Task management
- Employee portals
- Reporting systems
- Customer management
- Workflow automation

## Why Build a System?

A custom system can reduce:

- Manual data entry
- Spreadsheet dependency
- Duplicate records
- Repetitive tasks
- Communication gaps

## System Design

NEXA focuses on building systems around the actual workflow of the business rather than forcing businesses to adapt to generic software.
